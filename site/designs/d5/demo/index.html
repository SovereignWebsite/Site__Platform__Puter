Demo: ask a model with your own Puter account

This page is inert until you press a button. Nothing is billed to this site. Anything you use is charged to your own Puter allowance.

Before you start

  • The script is a third-party script by design: Puter.js 2.6.5, Puter's own npm package, served from this site as a pinned copy. This page's policy allows scripts from this site only.
  • Signing in opens a Puter window (or a tab, depending on your browser). That window is Puter's page, with Puter's own terms. We do not see your password.
  • Signing in gives this page a storage folder, a key-value store, AI access and the ability to publish websites on your account (Puter docs: Security and Permissions, read 25 November 2026). This demo uses only the AI call and your usage figure.
  • Type nothing private. Puter's published privacy policy does not say where AI prompts go.
  • The default model is Puter's own default (ai.chat() docs, read 25 November 2026). Your answer will be charged at that model's rate.

1 Load the script.

2 Ask. This signs you in with Puter if you are not already.


3 Optional: see your allowance.

Waiting. Nothing has run yet.

What to look for

  • If a pop-up is blocked, press the button again. Pop-ups need a click.
  • The usage result is Puter's raw answer. We do not convert its units: Puter's page for it does not give them.
  • If you see a 402 error, your balance may be used up, or several costly requests started together. A 429 means too many requests in a short time (rate limits).
