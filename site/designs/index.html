The five at a glance

Five designs
DesignIdeaBest forWeakest atDark first
1. LedgerA plain, printable document: one column, headings, links.Reading and printing; fastest to load.Little pull for a first-time visitor.no
2. PosterDark first: one big claim and one big button.Getting a visitor to try the demo.Heavy for long reading.yes
3. AtlasA map of the topic: side navigation, three entry cards.Visitors who know what they want to look up.Busy on a phone.no
4. PathA three-step journey drawn as a flow: visitor, Puter, model.Explaining who pays, at a glance.Less room for detail on the first screen.no
5. ConsoleA status panel first: what runs on load, what is allowed.Technical readers who want the facts of the page.Cold for non-technical visitors.no

Who recommends what

Keeper (puter.a), round 1: design 4, Path. It answers the question a visitor arrives with, "who pays?", before any text, and keeps the single action on the first screen. a.design has not reviewed this round yet.

How to choose

  1. Does the first screen on a phone say what this site is for?
  2. Can you reach the demo without hunting?
  3. Would the owner say "wow"?

Then say which number to keep.
